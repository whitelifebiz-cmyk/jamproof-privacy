2. What the app does

JamProof records your device's position during a car journey, at approximately one point per second, classifies each moment according to the measured speed, and produces a timestamped PDF report.

The classification thresholds are fixed and cannot be changed, either by you or by the publisher: flowing above 70 km/h (about 43 mph), slow between 30 and 70 (about 19 to 43 mph), jammed between 5 and 30 (about 3 to 19 mph), stopped below 5 (about 3 mph). That is what makes two reports comparable with one another.

3. What JamProof does not guarantee

Essential section. Read it before relying on a report to justify anything.

Recording can be interrupted without your knowing. Many phones stop background apps to save battery, under rules specific to each manufacturer, which the app neither controls nor can prevent. A trip may be only partly measured. This is why every report carries a GPS coverage indicator, and why the app displays a warning when that coverage is low. Check it before using a report.

The measurement depends on external conditions. Satellite signal quality varies with terrain, tunnels, covered car parks, buildings and weather. The values produced are measurements, with their margin of error, and not findings of fact.

The report has no evidentiary value. It attests to what the device measured, at the time it measured it. It proves neither the existence of a traffic jam, nor the reason for a delay, nor the identity of the driver, nor the route actually taken. Its acceptance rests entirely with the discretion of its recipient, whether an employer, an insurer, a public authority or a court.

Whitelife Studio cannot be held liable for the consequences of a report that is incomplete, inaccurate, refused or disputed, nor for a recording interrupted by your phone or by one of its settings, nor for the loss of data following an uninstall, a reset, a failure or a change of device. These limitations apply to the extent permitted by law. Nothing in these terms operates to exclude the rights granted to you by law as a consumer.
